# Rights, access status, and responsible use / 版权、获取状态与使用边界

## 中文

本仓库把“是否取得全文”和“是否拥有再分发权”视为两个不同问题。

- `全文`、`全文-reference`、`全文-预印本`、`全文-作者稿`或`全文-官方合订本`表示整理时本地文件包含完整内容，**不表示**仓库维护者拥有该论文版权，也不表示所有文件可按本项目许可再次传播。
- 文件名含 `NON_FULLTEXT` 的 PDF 是本项目制作的一页书目访问卡，不是论文、不是摘要替代品，也不是正式出版版本。访问卡提供 DOI、PMID、PMCID 或原始入口，方便读者从合法来源取得并核对原文。
- 论文、指南、作者稿、预印本以及出版社版本的权利仍由各自作者、学会、出版社或其他权利人持有。下载、分享和使用前，请以原始入口显示的许可、开放获取状态及所在地法律为准。
- MIT License 仅适用于本项目自有软件代码；CC BY 4.0 仅适用于本项目自有说明文字和整理后的书目元数据。二者都不覆盖第三方论文 PDF、期刊排版、图表、商标或其他第三方材料。
- 如权利人认为某个文件不应在本仓库中保存，请通过 GitHub Issue 指明文件路径和权利依据。维护者会优先核查，并在必要时移除文件、仅保留非全文访问卡及合法原始入口。
- 本项目仅用于文献发现、科研整理和教学，不构成医疗或法律建议。

## English

This repository treats “full text obtained” and “redistribution permission” as separate questions.

- An access value such as `全文` (full text), `全文-reference`, `全文-预印本` (preprint), `全文-作者稿` (author manuscript), or `全文-官方合订本` (official compilation) describes the local research artifact. It does **not** claim repository ownership of the article copyright or grant a blanket right to redistribute it.
- A PDF with `NON_FULLTEXT` in its filename is a one-page bibliographic access card authored by this project. It is not the paper, an abstract presented as a paper, or a version of record. It points readers to a DOI, PMID, PMCID, or original source entry.
- Rights in articles, guidelines, manuscripts, preprints, publisher layouts, figures, and related third-party material remain with their respective authors, societies, publishers, or other rightsholders. Check the license and access terms at the original source before downloading, sharing, or reusing content.
- The MIT License covers only repository-authored software. CC BY 4.0 covers only repository-authored documentation and the curated bibliographic metadata. Neither license covers third-party article PDFs, journal layouts, figures, trademarks, or other third-party material.
- A rightsholder who believes a file should not be stored here may open a GitHub Issue identifying the path and basis of the request. Maintainers should prioritize review and, where appropriate, remove the file while retaining only a non-full-text access card and lawful source links.
- This collection is for literature discovery, research organization, and education. It is not medical or legal advice.
