# Contributing / 参与维护

Corrections, missing literature, access-status updates, and metadata improvements are welcome.

欢迎提交文献补充、分类纠错、入口更新和元数据改进。

## Before opening an Issue or Pull Request

提交前请先搜索 README、CSV 和 JSON，避免引入重复记录。建议优先使用 DOI，其次使用 PMID、PMCID 或稳定的出版社入口作为唯一性依据。

Please search the README, CSV, and JSON first. Prefer DOI, followed by PMID, PMCID, or a stable publisher entry, when checking for duplicates.

## Required information / 必需信息

- Original title / 原始题名
- Publication year and journal / 年份与期刊
- DOI, PMID, PMCID, or original entry / DOI、PMID、PMCID 或原始入口
- Pediatric or adult classification, with a short rationale for mixed-age studies / 儿童或成人分类；混合年龄研究请说明依据
- Access status and its evidence / 获取状态及依据
- Whether it is an expert consensus statement or clinical guideline / 是否为专家共识或指南

## Full-text policy / 全文规则

Do not add an abstract, publisher preview, conference landing page, or bibliographic record as if it were the paper. If the complete article cannot be lawfully obtained, create or request a clearly marked `NON_FULLTEXT` access card and preserve the DOI, PMID, and original entry.

不得把摘要、出版社预览页、会议入口或书目记录冒充论文全文。无法合法取得完整文章时，应创建或申请醒目标注的 `NON_FULLTEXT` 访问卡，并保留 DOI、PMID 和原始入口。

By contributing repository-authored code, documentation, or metadata, you agree that it may be distributed under the repository licenses. Do not submit third-party material unless its inclusion and redistribution are permitted.
